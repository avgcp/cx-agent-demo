<objective>
Port the assessor briefing packet from chat to voice, so an escalated phone claim produces the
same six-section packet and delivers it as a real email — then ship it to the phone number only
after a human has heard the call.

Purpose: success criterion 3 is a property of the demo, not of one channel. Plans 05-04 and 05-05
proved the design on chat: an agent composes the packet, an agentTool exposes it, a separate tool
delivers it, and a gated step in `claim_intake` calls both. This plan is a port of that
known-good design onto the voice app, adapted for the two things voice is not — it has no photo
evidence, and everything it produces is spoken aloud.

Output: voice v12 cut and serving on `d28bbcb0` with the packet path proven on the phone, or
voice v11 `b17c9a26` still serving and a recorded failure.
</objective>

   after the repoint, including during Task 4.
9. **Quota budget: one API conversation of at most five turns, plus the one phone call in Task 4.**

10. **Determinism invariants that must survive, asserted on the same run:** the claim amount comes
